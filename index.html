01 — About

A little about me

I'm Wibhor — a CS student at Lovely Professional University, majoring in Data Science and Machine Learning. I'm in my second year, which means I'm at that perfect stage where I know enough to build things but still discover something new every week.

My approach is simple: pick something I don't know, build something with it, break it, figure out why it broke, fix it. That loop is how I've learned most of what I know.

Outside of code, I'm into cybersecurity (more of a "how does this actually work" interest than a career plan — yet), UI/UX design, games, and anime. I think good design and good code share the same quality: they both feel obvious in hindsight.

02 — Process

How I think

I learn by building, testing, breaking and improving.

01

Understand

What exactly am I trying to solve? Not just the surface request — the real problem underneath.

02

Break it down

Turn one big problem into smaller ones. Each piece becomes something I can actually work on.

03

Build

Start with the simplest working version. Don't overthink it — get something running first.

04

Break

Find what doesn't work. Test edge cases, stress it, use it wrong. That's where the learning is.

05

Improve

Make it cleaner, faster, and easier to use. Then start thinking about what's next.
